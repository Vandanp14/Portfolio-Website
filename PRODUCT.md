# Product truth — Vandan Patel portfolio

**Audience:** Engineering recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators.

**Job:** Let a visitor quickly assess Vandan's engineering focus and shipped work, then reach the detailed case studies, résumé, experience, or contact page.

**Positioning:** A student engineer who owns the path from data model and service to interface and deployment. The site must substantiate that claim with real project screens, implementation details, and sourced results.

**Voice:** First person, direct, technically specific, no inflated claims.

**Visual character:** Product-story driven, crisp near-white and near-black surfaces with a focused blue action color. Real interface screenshots lead the story; controls and route choices stay direct and easy to use.

**Primary action:** Explore work. **Secondary actions:** Contact, résumé.

**Preserve:** Verified facts and metrics; project screenshots; responsive navigation; all routes; keyboard access; reduced-motion support; theme choice; contact and résumé behavior.
